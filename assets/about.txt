# About CryptoVision 🚀

**CryptoVision** is a modern cryptocurrency market dashboard designed to make crypto market data simple, clear, and easy to understand.

The application provides real-time cryptocurrency market information such as **prices, market capitalization, 24-hour price changes, market performance, and interactive charts**. Users can explore popular cryptocurrencies and analyze their market movements through a clean and user-friendly dashboard.

## 🎯 Our Goal

The goal of CryptoVision is to provide an easy-to-use platform for exploring cryptocurrency market data without making the information unnecessarily complicated.

## ✨ Features

* 📊 Cryptocurrency market overview
* 💰 Current coin prices
* 📈 Interactive price charts
* 📉 Market performance analysis
* 💎 Market capitalization data
* 🔄 24-hour price changes
* 🌐 Cryptocurrency market statistics
* 📱 Clean and responsive dashboard

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

* **Python**
* **Streamlit**
* **Pandas**
* **Plotly**
* **CoinGecko API**
* **HTML & CSS**

## 📌 Project

CryptoVision is a learning and portfolio project focused on combining **Python, APIs, data analysis, and interactive dashboards** into a practical application.
